# Your records, on your server

ContextOrbit transfers the Health records, location observations and motion activity estimates you choose from your iPhone to one receiver you control. Review and analyze them on your computer with tools you choose.

## Before you use ContextOrbit

You provide a compatible receiver, its address and its access token. ContextOrbit does not include developer-operated storage or an app account. Your receiver's hosting, networking and backups are separate from the app.

## Reference receiver

The [reference receiver](https://github.com/yutashx/context-orbit-server) is software you may choose to run in your own environment. The developer does not host or manage your receiver or its data. You control installation, operation, access, backups, retention, deletion and any downstream use or sharing of records.

The receiver's MIT license applies to its published implementation, not the iPhone app. Receiver installation, behavior and limitations are documented in its own repository.
